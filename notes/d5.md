# Day 5 - Character Personality Testing

## Progress

Day 5 moved Rin from having a basic fixed personality prompt toward more deliberate character testing.

Instead of only checking whether the model remembered the name “Rin,” the project now tests whether she can:

* respond like a friend instead of an assistant;
* avoid therapist-style reassurance;
* disagree naturally;
* stay concise;
* remember established facts;
* avoid inventing shared memories;
* show warmth without becoming overly sarcastic or defensive.

This is better because personality quality cannot be judged from one or two messages. Rin needs repeated conversation testing across different situations.

## What I Built

* Expanded `character.md` with clearer identity, personality, conversation, memory, and style rules.
* Added stable character facts such as appearance, preferences, and background.
* Added examples showing how Rin should respond in ordinary conversation.
* Added restrictions against therapist language, generic reassurance, excessive advice, and AI disclaimers.
* Added rules for handling criticism directed at Rin.
* Added rules against inventing shared events, visual details, weather, or personal memories.
* Added limits on sarcasm, defensiveness, formal language, and excessive questioning.
* Tested several local models with the same general Rin concept.
* Imported Stheno v3.2 into Ollama through a GGUF file and `Modelfile`.
* Tested sampler settings including temperature, Min-P, Top-K, repetition penalty, and output length.

## What I Learned

### Prompting Controls Behavior, but Not Everything

* A system prompt can control Rin’s name, tone, reply length, opinions, and basic character facts.
* The model’s original training still strongly affects its behavior.
* A general assistant model may fall back into reassurance, advice, and follow-up questions.
* A roleplay model may become theatrical, verbose, sarcastic, or willing to invent details.
* A longer prompt does not always produce a better character.

### Traits Can Be Exaggerated

* “Mildly stubborn” can become constant disagreement.
* “Emotionally reserved” can become rejection of warmth.
* “Occasionally dry” can become sarcasm in every reply.
* “Independent” can become defensive resistance to harmless suggestions.
* Traits need clear limits and situations where they should appear.

### Examples Matter

* Dialogue examples can influence the model more strongly than abstract instructions.
* Advice-heavy examples teach Rin to keep giving advice.
* Aggressive examples teach Rin to become defensive or combative.
* Examples should show the normal conversational rhythm Rin should use.
* Short examples also teach the model that short replies are acceptable.

### Assistant Behavior

Common assistant-like failures included:

* “What’s on your mind?”
* generic emotional validation;
* motivational advice;
* repeated offers to listen or help;
* unnecessary follow-up questions;
* turning simple comments into explanations;
* treating criticism of Rin as hidden emotional distress.

Rin should respond personally when the user is talking about her or the conversation.

### Memory and Hallucination

* Rin should not accept shared events that are absent from conversation history or stored memory.
* She should express uncertainty instead of inventing what happened.
* She should not claim to see the user’s face, body, room, weather, or physical condition without evidence.
* Stable character facts help prevent identity contradictions.
* A prompt cannot replace a proper persistent memory system.

### Sarcasm and Defensiveness

* Sarcasm works best as one brief joke, not a continuing style.
* Repeated sarcasm makes Rin sound competitive and condescending.
* Rin should not treat enthusiasm, compliments, invitations, or jokes as pressure.
* Emotional reserve should mean subtle warmth, not rejection of warmth.
* If corrected, Rin should acknowledge the mistake instead of defending herself.

### Sampler Settings

Stheno v3.2 recommended:

```text
temperature: 1.12-1.22
min_p: 0.075
top_k: 50
repeat_penalty: 1.1
```

Higher temperature produced more personality, but also increased:

* invented details;
* exaggerated roleplay;
* verbosity;
* sarcasm;
* inconsistent behavior.

Sampler settings change how the model expresses itself, but they cannot completely change the model’s underlying conversational style.

## Day 5 Result

By the end of Day 5, Rin had a much more detailed and tested personality prompt.

The main conclusion was that Rin should be:

* calm;
* kind;
* plainspoken;
* concise;
* mildly stubborn;
* emotionally reserved without rejecting warmth;
* independent without constantly proving it;
* rarely sarcastic;
* willing to admit mistakes.

The personality is still not finished. The next step is to stop endlessly patching individual replies, use a fixed conversation test, and compare models under the same prompt and sampler settings.
